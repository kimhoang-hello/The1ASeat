# Post-game insights · V2.1

One Vietnamese insight is rendered per finished run, including fatal rounds. The score, rank, personal best, newly unlocked achievements and existing stats precede it. Play Again follows it. The program breakdown remains expandable. Social controls and image generation were removed from the running UI at the user's request. Existing challenge links still work.

## Editing

`src/recommendation-config.js` owns destinations, thresholds and copy. Its `annualFees` destination is deliberately null until a relevant article exists; the insight renders without a CTA. Other configured pages returned HTTP 200 on 5 September 2026. Only HTTPS destinations on ghe1a.com or www.ghe1a.com are accepted. Existing query parameters are retained alongside source=catch-the-points, medium=game, campaign=post-game and the hyphenated category in UTM parameters.

## Selection

`getPostGameRecommendation(stats)` in `src/recommendations.js` is pure and deterministic, including tie handling. It prioritizes:

1. Carrying a Balance always overrides everything.
2. Interest: at least two hits, 500 actual lost points, or at least 100 lost points representing 12% of potential earnings.
3. Expert: SEAT 1A or POINTS LEGEND, zero interest hits, combo at least 15 or final score at least 30,000, and damage no greater than 600 or 8% of potential earnings.
4. Largest meaningful actual penalty: at least 700 points, or at least 200 points representing 18% of potential earnings. Ties consistently favor devaluation, dynamic pricing, annual fees, then interest.
5. Strongest positive signal. Welcome bonuses count 1, golden bonuses 3, with a threshold of 4. Transfer signals combine bonus catches, partner boosts and earnings during selected partner boosts, with a threshold of 3 units (1 unit per 1,500 boost points). Award earnings require a triggered window and at least 1,800 earned points. Each signal is normalized by its threshold before comparison; ties favor welcome, transfer, then award.
6. Neutral Miles & Points fallback.

Potential earnings use total actual positive gains, with final score + tracked damage as a defensive lower bound. Positive signals are editorial selection weights, never score multipliers. Partner earnings include actual collected points during the selected active boost; they are not an estimate of the extra points the boost caused.

`Game.damage` records pre/post score differences after clamping, including both caught and surprise percentage devaluations. `Game.earnings` records actual positive gains, active award-window gains and selected partner-window gains. All reset with the existing round lifecycle. `buildRecommendationStats` combines these with existing counters, rank, best and finish reason. No extra randomness or scoring operations are introduced.

## Analytics

Listen for the existing `ghe1a:analytics` CustomEvent. No provider, network request, backend or personal data is added.

- `post_game_recommendation_shown`: once when the result insight renders.
- `post_game_recommendation_clicked`: on CTA activation; a single listener uses the current run's payload after replays.

Both include `recommendation_type`, `recommendation_category`, `tracking_id`, `final_score`, `rank` and `primary_gameplay_signal` (a name/value object). Provider exceptions cannot interrupt gameplay. The outgoing UTM link supports future website attribution; this update does not claim to measure downstream affiliate actions.
